# Combined All-Scene Feasibility

## Scope

Test whether the existing fixed reserve can satisfy all numerical base,
source, Count, family, and coverage requirements together, plus a diagnostic
overall multi-Selection target of 900 out of 3,600 Selection bases (25%).
This is not 25% in each split/depth or a G2:G3:G4 allocation.

Include the user-approved Unique depth-9/10 E decisions with requested-depth
two-member certificate support. Preserve the old frozen configuration and
scoped artifacts; use an explicitly nonfrozen diagnostic policy copy.

No new seeds, reserve refill, silent numeric quota changes, sealing, training,
or final replacement dataset export.

## Procedure

1. Reconstruct and preflight the unchanged complete source schedule and
   hashed development exclusions.
2. Fully re-admit every noncontrolled random-world, random-atoms, and targeted
   query slot under all-scene semantics, with paired explanations, context
   checks, requested token caps, and independent atomic artifact replay.
3. Check mandatory source supply before canonical deduplication. A shortage
   already disproves combined retention for the current recipes, because no
   other source can fill that source quota.
4. Check the optimistic multi-answer capacity allowed by the remaining Unique
   substantive-depth floors. If this bound is below 900, combined retention is
   impossible under the current requirements even with unlimited good
   controlled recipes.
5. If necessary gates pass, map existing controlled slots to repaired recipes,
   including one deterministic Selection cardinality per fixed slot, not extra
   renamed bases. Check canonical scene isolation across the complete schedule
   and explicitly reject overlap slots.
6. Fully re-admit controlled candidates, deduplicate canonical query identities,
   and solve retention with every existing quota/coverage/family requirement
   and the exact overall 900-base multi-Selection target.
7. Independently validate any selected subset. A solver timeout is not evidence
   of infeasibility.

Positive supervision remains entailment-only. Negative possibilities remain
diagnostics, not answer targets. E/F are separate from substantive G1-G4.

The proposed experimental controlled supply mapping makes approximately half of existing
Selection slots multi-answer, with deterministic 2-4 cardinalities, to provide
retention choices. This is not a final retained proportion or an approved
G2:G3:G4 allocation. Only the overall retained 25% is tested.

## Result

**The combined 25% target is incompatible with the current in-range Unique
substantive-depth minima and verified noncontrolled source inventory.**
The completed receipt is `COMBINED-ALL-SCENE-FEASIBILITY-v1.json`.

The original 18,290-story schedule passed preflight. All 7,632 noncontrolled
query slots were checked:

| Outcome | Slots |
|---|---:|
| Accepted and independently replayed | 3,928 |
| Rejected for unmentioned scene objects | 3,540 |
| Unsupported evidence/context invariance | 164 |

There were no independent verification failures. Every mandatory noncontrolled
source quota has enough raw verified supply before deduplication. Maximum
target lengths were 779 Natural and 962 Symbolic tokens.

## Capacity Bound

The old train/dev/in-range Unique minima still require substantive A-D
answers with depth support. A deep multiple-entailment E decision cannot count
toward those minima. Only the extrapolation rule has been revised by approval.

| Split | Controlled Selection Quota | Unique Substantive Minimum | Optimistic Noncontrolled Substantive Supply | Optimistic Noncontrolled Multi Supply | Total Multi Upper Bound |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Train | 1,400 | 1,400 | 2 | 122 | 124 |
| Dev | 280 | 280 | 0 | 28 | 28 |
| In-range test | 560 | 560 | 1 | 45 | 46 |
| Extrapolation | 400 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 400 |
| Overall | | | | | 598 |

For train, at least 1,398 controlled bases must contribute substantive Unique
depth support because noncontrolled sources can contribute at most two.
Only two of the 1,400 controlled slots can therefore be multi-answer, plus at
most 122 noncontrolled multi-answer bases: an optimistic bound of 124.
The same reasoning applies to the other splits.

The noncontrolled substantive column counts bases satisfying the current depth
metadata requirement, not every singleton gold answer. The bound caps each
source by its retained source quota and deliberately ignores deduplication,
other coverage constraints, family floors, and simultaneous competition for
source slots. It therefore overestimates, rather than understates, possible
multi-answer capacity.

At most 598 of 3,600 Selection bases could be multi-answer under this bound,
below the requested 900. This is not an achieved retained set or an assertion
that 598 is attainable.

## Execution Boundary

The long controlled-admission phase was stopped after this necessary
infeasibility bound was established. No full retention MILP was needed or run.
The receipt was finalized from the complete source metadata after checking its
SHA-256, exact fixed-slot inventory, token-admission flags, and independent
replay markers. Its `seconds` field measures receipt finalization, not the
earlier complete source scan.

The proposed next revision is to apply requested-depth **Unique decision
support** to train, dev, and in-range minima too, counting deep multiple-
entailment E certificates as well as substantive singleton certificates.
This would keep the numeric depth minimum but change what qualifies. It has
not been approved or applied.

The earlier controlled Count supply repair remains valid. No new seeds,
numeric quota relaxation, replacement dataset, sealing, or training occurred.
Full combined retention after any further policy revision remains untested.
