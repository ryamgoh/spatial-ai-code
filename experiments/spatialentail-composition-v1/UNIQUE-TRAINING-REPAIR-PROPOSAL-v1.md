# Unique Training Repair Proposal

Status: approved bounded repair. The user chose **Approve both repairs**:
the selected-explanation depth amendment and one batch capped at 2,048 candidate
queries. This authorizes the specified repair only, with no automatic refill,
evaluation changes, sealing or training. The original proposal below records
the design and its limitations; approval is not a claim of successful selection.

## Preserve The Study

Keep 6,000 distinct training questions, one selected menu each, and matched
Natural/Symbolic evidence. Keep the approved E subtypes, existing controlled
L1-L8 weights, Count-value quotas, family floors and negative-evidence minima.
Keep the 70/15/10/5 source mix. Do not modify any evaluation selection, its
L1-L10 allocation, or the audited external Unique set.

## Selected-Explanation Depth

Propose replacing only the three training-wide primary-menu depth predicates
with a selected-explanation proof predicate, keeping the numerical minimum
at 1,400 per query.

- Direction/Count A-D or F: the selected explanation must contain a verified
  positive proof attaining the recorded calculus-relative support depth.
  F must also establish that the sole value is missing from the menu.
- Which A-D or F: retain full-domain uniqueness evidence, including checked
  non-entailment of every alternative, plus the qualifying positive depth proof.
- Which multiple-entailment E: retain the verified cheapest two-member decision
  certificate at the recorded depth. Do not substitute an arbitrarily deeper pair.
- No-entailment and inconsistent E receive no invented support-depth credit.
- Compute this predicate from evidence and the selected explanation. Do not
  copy a primary-row flag onto F, or rewrite historical measurements.

The rule change applies to training only. Keep per-depth family and
nonhomogeneous-proof minima unchanged initially, including their current menu
predicates; the joint selection must establish that these remain feasible.
If they fail, report that conflict rather than weakening them automatically.

Which also needs this change for the aggregate repair table below. Its controlled
cohort contains 1,229 sole-entailment questions, exceeding the entire 1,200 A-D
allocation; at least 29 controlled questions must therefore use F. Excluding
them merely because they are omission menus would lose genuine proof exposure.

## Proposed Bounded Execution

Subject to separate approval, use one fixed supplementary manifest with a hard
cap of **2,048 query admissions**, allocated as follows. This is a resource cap,
not an empirically calibrated success guarantee or an open-ended refill loop.

| Query | Random-world attempts | Random-atoms attempts | Targeted attempts | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Direction | 768 | 192 | 64 | 1,024 |
| Count | 192 | 192 | 0 | 384 |
| Which | 384 | 256 | 0 | 640 |
| Total | 1,344 | 640 | 64 | 2,048 |

Before execution, record exact seeds and query slots in an immutable new
manifest, check them against old inventories, and preserve existing family
assignment and overlap exclusions. A family assigned to evaluation is rejected,
not moved to training. Record every attempted slot and rejection.

Keep actual random-world and random-atoms generation random; do not relabel
hand-constructed cases as random sources. Use the existing random samplers first.
For targeted Direction ambiguity, inspect the existing contingent construction
under all-scene admission before adopting it; its pattern name is not proof of
its semantic outcome or independent family supply.

Only admit the declared query slots. Different query types may share an eligible
scene, but no question, renaming or menu permutation counts twice. Keep existing
seeded omission eligibility; do not generate extra menus to fill F silently.
Reject unsupported proofs, unknown results, overlaps and token failures.

After the bounded batch, run joint one-menu selection with exact answer and
E-subtype quotas, all retained source/depth/Count/family/negative-evidence gates,
and selected-explanation depth measurement. Independently validate the chosen
set against the unchanged evaluation sets. Stop on failure and report supply;
no second manifest or automatic refill is authorized by this proposal.

Only a validated selection proceeds to short-protocol reference materialization,
token checks, independent audit and prose review. Sealing and training still
require separate approval.
