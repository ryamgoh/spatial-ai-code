# All-Scene Gold Balance Feasibility

## Scope

This is a bounded development check of the proposed 25% multi-entailed Selection
target. It is not an allocation approval, production reserve expansion, new
dataset release, or training authorization.

G1-G4 count selected substantive A-D letters; E/F remain separate escape
categories. The 25% proposal concerns Selection bases with exactly 2-4 entailed
objects. It does not concern 25% of Unique outputs or 25% of all output views.
The same multi-answer base makes Unique return E and permits Listed/Exhaustive
G2-G4 when the complete set is displayed.

## Construction

The development-only `all-scene-cardinality-probe-v1` recipe reuses the existing
forest scaffold, but changes how positive endpoint support is constructed.
At depths 2-10 it separates the horizontal and vertical supports. Intermediate
objects on the long path are provably outside the queried direction; only the
intended 1-4 endpoint objects qualify. For cardinal directions, the short
alignment path passes through an opposite-side object instead of adding a
shallow positive intermediate.

Every named nonreference object is queried. There is no hidden candidate subset.
All positive members must have the requested shortest-axis support depth.
For multi-answer cases, the cheapest two positive certificates must also have
that depth, so Unique does not borrow difficulty from a farther answer.
These are calculus-relative support checks, not universal minimum semantic
reasoning-depth claims.

The existing seeded negative forest supplies structural variation. An attached
context object remains explicitly excluded; its additional relation can be
removed without changing the entailed set. No distractor quota was introduced.
Question premises remain an unnumbered paragraph using ordinary place names.
Premise labels are defined inside matched Natural/Symbolic explanations.

## Bounded Check

The fixed diagnostic schedule contains:

- One scaffold seed across 10 depths, eight directions and four cardinalities:
  320 bases.
- Seven additional scaffold seeds at depths 1, 4, 8 and 10, in North and Northeast,
  for all four cardinalities: 224 bases.
- Total: 544 development bases, with related variants grouped into their
  scaffold family rather than treated as independent families.

Checks include consistency, the complete entailed set, full-domain negative
counterexamples, shortest member support, Unique's two-member certificate,
premise-removal dependence, renaming/order invariance, context removal, matched
contract labels, omission companions, paragraph parsing and pinned-tokenizer
budgets. The independent atomic auditor replays labels, positive paths and
negative coordinates without using the production spatial solver or encoder.

Forced omission companions exercise the contracts in this check. They do not
change the approved production omission probability of 20%.

## Allocation Boundary

For 2,000 training Selection bases, 25% means 500 multi-entailed bases.
If these were supplied entirely by the 70% controlled source component, they
would occupy 500 of its 1,400 Selection bases, approximately 35.7%.
This is arithmetic, not a demonstrated retained supply or an applied quota.

Constructing examples at every depth cannot by itself establish:

- The required number of families at every training and evaluation depth.
- Family isolation across train, dev, in-range test and extrapolation.
- Supply within the unchanged fixed reserve and approved source proportions.
- Simultaneous satisfaction of the existing semantic coverage requirements.
- Controlled Count targets under all-scene scope.

The eventual retention check must demonstrate those together. Renaming and
option-position variants cannot replace independent base/family support.

## Results

All 544 scheduled development bases passed. The complete evidence is in
[ALL-SCENE-BALANCE-FEASIBILITY-v1.json](ALL-SCENE-BALANCE-FEASIBILITY-v1.json).
The run took approximately 175 seconds on this machine; this is not a production
runtime estimate.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, 4, 8, 10 | 22/22 | 22/22 | 22/22 | 22/22 | 8 |
| 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 | 8/8 | 8/8 | 8/8 | 8/8 | 1 |

Every cell includes all eight directions. The extra seven scaffold families
were checked in North/Northeast at the four selected depths, not all directions.
All positive member supports and all multi-answer Unique certificates matched
the nominated depth. Independent atomic label/proof/counterexample replay passed
for every base. Forced omission companions passed for every base.

Maximum target tokens: Natural 1,406; Symbolic 1,636. All checked views fit the
pinned tokenizer budgets. Maximum scene size was 24 objects. The entire
entailment regression suite passed: 633 tests. Changed-code Ruff checks pass.
These automated checks do not constitute full manual prose-quality review.

An 80-base diagnostic subset contains six singleton and two multi-answer bases
at each of the ten depths: 20/80, exactly 25%. G2, G3 and G4 are represented
across the schedule, rather than restricting all multi-answer examples to
shallow depths. This subset uses one scaffold family and is explicitly not a
production dataset or evidence of independent-family supply. E/F cases were not
allocated in this miniature subset; it demonstrates cardinality/depth
compatibility, not the complete final output distribution.

**Conclusion:** 25% is constructively feasible for all-scene Selection at the
tested depths, without making intermediate objects unintended positive answers
or giving Unique a shallow two-answer certificate. There is no observed
construction or token-budget barrier in this probe.

**Not yet established:** retention of 500 multi-answer training bases while
simultaneously satisfying the unchanged source mixture, per-depth family floors,
split isolation, coverage requirements and fixed reserve. The probe has eight
families, not the full production family inventory. The unchanged production
admission still rejects multi-answer Unique E unless the earlier opt-in
decision-depth policy is enabled; no configuration was changed here.

Recommended next step: test the existing fixed scaffold inventory and retention
constraints using this construction, then review a simple G2/G3/G4 allocation
before authorizing a replacement run. Do not silently refill the reserve or
reinterpret old scoped labels.
