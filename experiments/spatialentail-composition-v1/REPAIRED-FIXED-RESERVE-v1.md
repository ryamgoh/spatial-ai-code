# Repaired Fixed-Reserve Feasibility

## Purpose

Adapt the controlled construction to all-scene semantics using the existing
fixed slots and seeds. Preserve the frozen configuration, original artifacts,
source allocation, and exclusion inventory. No final replacement dataset,
sealing, or training is authorized by this check.

Renamed questions are useful presentation variants, but do not count as
additional independent bases or families in this feasibility audit.

## Recipe

`all-scene-forest-v1` uses direct atomic facts at depth 1 and joint two-axis
supports at depths 2-10. Supporting objects are excluded from the queried
direction unless they are intended answers. Positive endpoints share terminal
support edges for cardinalities 2-4 without direct-to-reference shortcuts.
Count zero uses negative membership supports rather than positive endpoints.

A named context object remains in the scene with a direct opposite-direction
fact. At depths above 1, a redundant extra context fact is marked removable and
its removal is checked against the full all-scene answer.

Count slots keep their original intended values 0-4. The fixed mapping does not
increase the number of slots or add fresh seeds. Alternative Selection
cardinalities are explicitly development probes, not extra retained bases or
an approved G2:G3:G4 allocation.

## Family and Identity Checks

The mapping preserves original source lineage and conservatively merges
families if their repaired anchor constructions are equivalent. Such a merge
must never cross splits. The complete mapped schedule also undergoes canonical
story split-isolation and development-exclusion checks.

The Count supply check removes canonical query duplicates. Pure object
renaming, premise ordering, or option positioning cannot inflate supply.

## Verification

The initial reserve audit stopped at mapping preflight.
`REPAIRED-FIXED-RESERVE-v1.json` records the failed check: related repaired
construction families crossed splits. No Count admission or retention was
performed after that failure.

Inspection found 38 cross-split repaired-anchor groups. Rebuilding the forest
with the larger two-axis support had truncated negative branches, erasing
distinctions between original families.

The revised `all-scene-forest-v2` preserves the original negative forest and
replaces only the answer-support portion. A direct inventory check of all
1,707 selected fixed seeds found 1,707 repaired anchors and zero cross-split
anchor groups. The complete revised audit is separately recorded in
`REPAIRED-FIXED-RESERVE-v2.json` and reviewed in
`REPAIRED-FIXED-RESERVE-v2.md`.

All mapped Count slots are checked for their exact intended all-scene value,
requested support depth, context invariance, and independent SMT entailment.
Count membership certificates are independently replayed.

One Count slot per split/depth/direction/value and one Selection slot per
split/depth/direction at each cardinality 1-4 receive full paired-reference,
forced omission, independent artifact, and pinned-tokenizer checks. Those
Selection alternatives do not establish retained multi-answer base supply.

The initial recipe and production regression run passed 172 tests, and the
initial full entailment regression run passed 801 tests. Revised forest
preservation passed 163 semantic/production cases and seven focused mapping,
family-preservation, bounds, and policy-conflict tests.

## Existing Requirement Conflict

Even a perfect generator cannot satisfy the following unchanged requirements
together:

- Extrapolation contains 400 Selection bases.
- Applying 25% multi-answer coverage within extrapolation would make 100 of
  them have 2-4 entailed objects.
- Unique correctly returns E for those 100 bases.
- The old frozen rule requires 400 primary Unique Selection answers with
  substantive depth support, meaning substantive A-D output.

That leaves at most 300 substantive Unique answers, not the required 400.
This is an arithmetic conflict, not a lack of renamed examples or a solver
failure. The frozen rule has not been altered.

This is a conditional per-split example. No per-split 25% requirement has been
approved. Overall 25% coverage could instead concentrate multi-answer bases in
other splits while leaving extrapolation singleton-only; that allocation also
needs review and verification.

A reviewable alternative is to preserve the requirement for deep reasoning on
all 400 Unique decisions, using decision-depth support for multi-entailment E
answers and substantive support for singleton answers. This is a proposal,
not an applied coverage-policy change.

## Remaining Boundary

Passing the controlled check is not full replacement-dataset feasibility.
Random and targeted sources still need all-scene re-admission; combined exact
retention, coverage, family floors, and any proposed 25% allocation remain to
be tested after the incompatible Unique requirement is resolved.
